Project 02 · Product strategy

Taking a biofeedback product from laptop to mobile

Led the move of an rPPG biofeedback product to mobile, balancing user needs, feasibility, and signal accuracy.

Role
Software Engineer Intern, Product Strategy
Organization
Streamind Health
Timeline
Oct 2025 – May 2026
Focus
Product strategy

Context

Streamind Health makes a biofeedback product built on rPPG, which reads pulse signals from camera video. The product already worked on laptops, and the goal was to bring it to mobile.

What I did

  1. Led the laptop-to-mobile rollout, weighing user needs, feasibility, and signal accuracy.
  2. Evaluated camera performance and signal-quality tradeoffs to guide product and engineering decisions.
  3. Built data capture and visualization features that turn physiological signals into information for therapists and patients.

Results

Led the laptop-to-mobile rollout
Built data capture and visualization features for therapists and patients
