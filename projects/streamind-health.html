What I worked on

Streamind Health makes a biofeedback tool built on rPPG, which estimates pulse signals from camera video. It already worked on laptops, and I helped adapt it for mobile.

What I did

  • Helped adapt the tool from laptop to mobile, weighing user needs, feasibility, and signal accuracy.
  • Evaluated camera performance and signal-quality tradeoffs to guide product and engineering decisions.
  • Built data capture and visualization features that turn physiological signals into information for therapists and patients.

Methods and tools

  • Product strategy
  • Tradeoff analysis
  • Data visualization
