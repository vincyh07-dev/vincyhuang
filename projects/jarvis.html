Jarvis

Cal Hacks 13.0Team project

My team built an AI meeting assistant that joins Google Meet, answers questions out loud, and keeps track of what the team decides.

Watch demo (YouTube, opens in a new tab) View on Devpost (opens in a new tab)

Why we built it

My team built Jarvis for student clubs planning events. We wanted teams to keep track of decisions and bring absent teammates into the conversation while the meeting was still happening.

What it does

Jarvis joins Google Meet, answers questions out loud, and keeps a live whiteboard of decisions and action items. Teammates who miss the call can ask for a recap on Slack or send a message back to the group.

What we learned

When we demoed Jarvis, people started imagining how they’d use it in their own work meetings. They asked about automatic joining, asking questions through chat, and whether Jarvis could follow a screen share. That gave us ideas for what to build next.
