Project 04 · User research

How students and faculty use LLM grading and feedback tools

Focus groups and surveys on LLM feedback tools, turned into memos that help educators choose what works.

Role
Undergraduate Research Assistant, Data Analytics
Organization
UC Berkeley Center for Teaching & Learning
Timeline
Jun 2026 – Present
Focus
User research

Context

This is ongoing research on how students and faculty use LLM tools for grading and feedback, so educators can teach better and choose effective learning tools.

What I did

  1. Run focus groups and surveys with students and faculty.
  2. Analyze qualitative and quantitative feedback for patterns in engagement, usability, and learning needs.
  3. Turn the findings into stakeholder memos for educators.

Results

Stakeholder memos delivered to help educators improve teaching and choose learning tools
Ongoing: no final results to report yet

  • Surveys
  • Mixed-methods analysis
