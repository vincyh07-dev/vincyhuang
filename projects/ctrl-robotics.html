Making hospital robot navigation easier to use

Turned hospital operators' needs into product requirements, then redesigned the robot's mapping and navigation tools.

Role
Software Engineer Intern, Product & Operations
Organization
CTRL Robotics
Timeline
Jan – May 2026
Focus
Product

Context

CTRL Robotics builds an autonomous robotics platform for hospitals. I worked on the product and operations side, starting from how hospital teams actually work.

What I did

  1. Analyzed hospital workflows and turned operator needs into product requirements.
  2. Redesigned the mapping and navigation tools.
  3. Worked with engineers to launch robot-selection and badge-registration features that simplified clinical operations.

Results

25%improvement in usability of mapping and navigation tools
30%reduction in navigation errors
Robot-selection and badge-registration features launched with the engineering team
